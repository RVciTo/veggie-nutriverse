# Experiment: Nutriverse, get the home page indexed

Written by the CGO, run cgo-handback.needs/2026-10-10T0709, from the CPO daily read of 10 Oct 2026.


Issue: https://github.com/RVciTo/veggie-nutriverse/issues/19

- Hypothesis: If the home page is submitted for indexing and the sitemap is read, Google starts
  showing it, because the site has 0 visitors in 28 days and 0 Google impressions on every day
  from 29 Sep to 6 Oct, which reads as not indexed.
- Type: content/channel (SEO, indexing). No spend, no public words.
- Primary metric: Google impressions above 0 on any day (Search Console, via `nutriverse-web.json`).
- Window: 10 to 24 Oct. Read on the file of 24 Oct.
- Steps for the caller (needs web-developer or the indexing loop, not the cgo): Search Console URL
  inspection of the home page, request indexing, check the sitemap is submitted and the page is not
  noindex or blocked in robots.
- Stop rule: 24 Oct. Impressions above 0: win, record it and keep the site. Still 0: close as a loss
  and hand back to the CPO, the question of keeping the site live returns to Hervé.

## Result (27 Oct 2026, run cgo-handback.needs/2026-10-27T0705): loss, stop

- Search Console impressions were 0 on every day read from 29 Sep to 23 Oct (files
  `S/insights/<day>/nutriverse-web.json`, GSC day lags by 3 days; 24 Oct not yet in a file). Clicks 0,
  GA4 sessions 0 on 26 Oct.
- The sitemap is submitted, 0 errors, and Google last read it on 24 Oct (the file of 20 Oct still
  showed 13 Oct). So Google fetches the sitemap but shows nothing: the page is not served in results yet.
- Decision per the stop rule: stop this test, do not extend it. Verdict: loss (metric not met).
- Next step: the question of keeping the site live goes back to the CPO and then Hervé (not the cgo).
  Recommendation: keep it for now at no cost and run no further indexing experiment until the site has
  content worth ranking; a new test needs a new hypothesis (for example a page with a real query target).
- CPO addendum (27 Oct, run cpo-handback.needs/2026-10-27T0706), from `~/.odyssey/logs/webops-index.log`:
  the indexing request was never sent. Every try from 10 to 25 Oct ended in ERROR (Chrome not signed in to
  the Search Console property, or two Chrome browsers connected) or QUOTA; no REQUESTED line exists. So the
  hypothesis (request, then index, then impressions) was never tested as written.
- Secondary metric met after the end date: on 26 Oct 08:25 UTC URL Inspection reports the home page
  "Submitted and indexed" (PASS); Google indexed it on its own after reading the sitemap on 24 Oct.
  Impressions after 24 Oct are not in S yet (GSC lags 3 days).
- Verdict stands under the stop rule (primary metric 0 on 24 Oct: loss). The question of keeping the site
  live goes to Hervé as a Decision issue, with the recommendation to keep it at no cost and re-read
  impressions from S on 9 Nov, now that the page is indexed.
