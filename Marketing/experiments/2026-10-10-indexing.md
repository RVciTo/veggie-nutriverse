- Hypothesis: If the home page is submitted for indexing and the sitemap is read, Google starts
  showing it, because the site has 0 visitors in 28 days and 0 Google impressions on every day
  from 29 Sep to 6 Oct, which reads as not indexed.
- Type: content/channel (SEO, indexing). No spend, no public words.
- Primary metric: Google impressions above 0 on any day (Search Console, via `nutriverse-web.json`).
- Window: 10 to 24 Oct. Read on the file of 24 Oct.
- Steps for the caller (needs web-developer or the indexing loop, not the cgo): Search Console URL
  inspection of the home page, request indexing, check the sitemap is submitted and the page is not
  noindex or blocked in robots.
- Stop rule: 24 Oct. Impressions above 0: win, record it and keep the site. Still 0: close as a loss
  and hand back to the CPO, the question of keeping the site live returns to Hervé.
